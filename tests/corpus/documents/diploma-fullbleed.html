Modules and assessment

The summer school combines field work with critiques in the studio. Each participant keeps a daily log of the conditions, the equipment and the decisions behind each picture; the tutors comment on it every evening. The assessment considers the portfolio of twelve prints, the log and a short presentation at the exhibition.

Grades run from 1 (excellent) to 5 (not passed). A distinction is awarded where the average is 1.5 or better and no module is graded worse than 2. The diploma is recognised by the Norwegian association of nature photographers as part of its professional training.
