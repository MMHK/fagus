Reorder Points under Uncertain Demand

Mira Okafor

2 November 2026

Abstract

We derive the reorder point ss of a continuous-review policy when the daily demand dd is normally distributed and the lead time LL is fixed, and compare it with the order quantity of the classic economic order model.

1 Model

Let the demand per day have the mean μ\mu and the standard deviation σ\sigma. Over a lead time of LL days the demand DL=∑i=1LdiD_L = \sum_{i=1}^L d_i has the mean μL=Lμ\mu_L = L\mu and the variance σL2=Lσ2\sigma_L^2 = L\sigma^2. The reorder point covers this demand with the service level α\alpha:

s=μL+zα⋅Lσ2,Φ(zα)=12π∫−∞zαe−t2/2dt=α.s = \mu_L + z_\alpha \cdot \sqrt{L\sigma^2}, \qquad \Phi(z_\alpha) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{z_\alpha} e^{-t^2/2}\,dt = \alpha.

The safety stock S=s−μLS = s - \mu_L grows with the square root of the lead time, not with the lead time itself. With the ordering cost KK, the holding cost hh and the annual demand DD, the order quantity is

Q*=2KDh,C(Q)=KDQ+hQ2.Q^* = \sqrt{\frac{2KD}{h}}, \quad C(Q) = \frac{KD}{Q} + \frac{hQ}{2}.
