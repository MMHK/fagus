Regional sales, Q1–Q3¶

This notebook loads the order export of the shop, cleans the net_amount column and compares the three regions month by month. The figures are net of VAT and in euros; refunds are booked in the month of the refund, not of the order.

  • Source: exports/orders-2025-09-30.csv (41 208 rows)
  • Regions: Nord, Süd and West
    • Austria and Switzerland count as Süd

           .groupby([orders.booked_at.dt.to_period("M"), "region"])["net_amount"]
           .agg(["count", "sum", "mean"])
