Warranty terms

The contractor warrants that all work will be performed in a good and workmanlike manner and in accordance with the contract documents, and that all materials and equipment furnished will be new and of good quality unless otherwise required or permitted. The warranty period is five years from the date of acceptance for structural work and two years for all other work. Defects notified within the warranty period shall be remedied without charge within a reasonable time, not exceeding thirty days for defects that impair the use of the building.

This warranty does not cover damage resulting from abuse, modifications not executed by the contractor, improper or insufficient maintenance, improper operation, or normal wear and tear under normal usage.
