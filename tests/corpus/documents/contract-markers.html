  3. Support services

    1. The Licensor provides the following services during the term:

      • services required because the Licensee modified the Software;

  4. Fees and payment

    1. The annual licence and support fee is stated in Annex 1. It is due annually in advance and payable within 30 days of invoice.
    2. The Licensor may adjust the fee once per calendar year by notice of three months, at most by the change of the index of labour costs published by the Federal Statistical Office.

  5. Term and termination

    1. This agreement enters into force on 1 January 2027 and runs for an initial term of three years. It is extended by one year at a time unless terminated with six months' notice to the end of a term.
    2. The right of either party to terminate for good cause remains unaffected. Good cause exists for the Licensor in particular if
      1. the Licensee is in default with payment of two annual fees;
      2. the Licensee materially breaches clause 2.3 and does not cease to do so within 30 days of a written warning.

  6. Final provisions

    1. Amendments must be made in writing. This also applies to this written form clause.
    2. German law applies. The courts of Hamburg have exclusive jurisdiction.
