Quiet

The magnetic drive runs below 28 dB(A) at nominal flow, so the pump can be installed in living areas without extra insulation.

Efficient

An electronically commutated motor adapts its speed to the demand and uses as little as 4 W in night mode.

Durable

Ceramic bearings and a stainless steel housing are rated for more than 60,000 operating hours in hard water.
