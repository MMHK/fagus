Regional failover v4.12.0

Projects can now fail over between regions automatically. When health checks in eu-central-1 fail three times in a row, traffic moves to eu-west-2 within 30 seconds. Configure it per environment:

$ acme failover enable --primary eu-central-1 --secondary eu-west-2 \
    --threshold 3 --interval 10s
✓ Failover enabled for production (Δ 0.4 ms p50 latency)

Labels use Small Caps for Units and Abbreviations; ligatures are off in identifiers like office_fi_ffl but on in prose such as “office affiliate”. Kerning: AVATAR Yo Te versus AVATAR Yo Te.
