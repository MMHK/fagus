Churn by plan¶

Monthly churn of paying accounts, October 2025. A customer churns when the subscription ends without renewal; downgrades are not churn. Source: billing.subscriptions.

  • accounts younger than 30 days are left out
  • colours: blue is below, red above the company target of 2.5 %
