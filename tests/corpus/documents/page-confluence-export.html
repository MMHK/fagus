Summary

Between 13:42 and 15:09 UTC, payment confirmations reached merchants with a delay of up to 74 minutes. No payments were lost and no customer was charged twice, but 1,836 orders stayed in the state pending_confirmation longer than our SLA of five minutes allows.

Merchants on the legacy plan received no retry e-mails during the incident. Customer support contacted the 42 affected merchants on October 22.

Root cause

The new release lowered max_idle_connections from 64 to 8 while keeping the number of worker threads. Under the afternoon peak, workers waited for connections and the retry back-off multiplied the delay:

dispatcher.yaml (v2.31.0)
http:
  max_idle_connections: 8      # was 64
  idle_timeout: 30s
workers: 48
retry:
  initial_backoff: 2s
  multiplier: 2.0
  max_backoff: 10m

Five whys

  1. Why were webhooks delayed? Workers waited for HTTP connections.
  2. Why did they wait? The pool allowed only eight idle connections for 48 workers.
  3. Why was the pool reduced? To lower memory use after the move to smaller containers.
  4. Why was the effect not caught? The load test runs with 10 % of production traffic.
  5. Why so little? The test environment has not been resized since 2024.

What went well: the alert fired within twelve minutes and the rollback took less than five.

Action items

  • Roll back webhook-dispatcher to v2.30.4 Tomás Ferreira
  • Contact the affected merchants on the legacy plan Ana Lindqvist
  • Size the pool from the number of workers instead of a constant Priya Raman 15 Nov 2026
