Summary

The third quarter was dominated by the heating changeover in hall B and the move of the dispatch office. Ticket volume rose slightly, mainly through planned work orders; reactive tickets fell by eleven per cent. Two incidents breached the resolution target, both caused by a delayed spare part for the dock leveller at gate 7.

Planned maintenance now makes up 58 % of all work orders, the highest share since the contract began.

Main activities

  1. Replacement of the gas condensing boiler in hall B (completed 14 August).
  2. Relocation of the dispatch office including network and access control.
  3. Quarterly inspection of fire doors, sprinklers and emergency lighting.
    • 17 fire doors adjusted, 2 closers replaced
    • emergency lighting battery test passed
Open item: the roof drainage of hall A needs cleaning before the first frost; quotation follows separately.
