Resetting your workspace

Help centre · Updated 30 October 2026 · 4 min read

If your workspace becomes slow or shows stale data, you can reset it. Press Ctrl + Shift + R to open the reset panel. The SSO session stays active.

What does a reset remove?

A reset removes cached files, local drafts and unsaved filters. Shared dashboards and comments are kept on the server.

Can I undo a reset?

No. This paragraph is hidden in print because the element is closed.

Who can reset a team workspace?

Only owners and administrators. Members can reset their personal workspace only.
