Before your first day

  • Sign the contract and the confidentiality agreement
  • Send your tax ID and bank details to HR
  • Read the support handbook (about 40 minutes)
    • Chapter 3: tone of voice
  • Set up two-factor authentication for Google, Zendesk and 1Password

Tools

Daily

  • Zendesk for tickets and macros
  • Slack, channels #support and #support-urgent
  • Stripe (read only) to look up payments

Weekly

  1. Team call on Monday 10:00
  1. Ticket review with your buddy
  1. Friday: write down one thing that confused you

  • How do I write a good internal note?

    State what the customer wants, what you checked and what you did. Link the order. Someone else will read it at 7 a.m.

    Customer wants: refund for damaged mug (order #48211)
Checked: photo attached, delivered 2 days ago
Did: refund 14,90 € via Stripe, macro refund-approved
  • Holiday cover rules

    Hidden while collapsed.

Escalation

If a customer mentions a lawyer, the press or a safety issue, stop and escalate.
